## The locked line

The entry is a workplace rant about the corporate passive-aggression of
"per my last email": the narrator pretends the original email was carved
into stone tablets and she chose illiteracy, then reads it twice and
concludes it still says nothing, only louder. The joke lands in the
escalation — stone tablets, chosen illiteracy, twice-read — and the kicker
("only louder") reframes the empty email as a volume knob rather than
information.
